Hi, I'm Cosy

About

This is Cosy

Cosy is the character I use, and she appears in all my works and projects.

That's why I'll use the word “our”, because there are two of us.

Our goal

Our goal is to make the world more cosy. We want everyone to be able to be themselves, enjoy life and do what they want, what they really want. It's very simple: everyone should pursue their dream and be happy.

Our philosophy

Be cosy rules

Three rules. Follow them and you're living a cosy life.

Don't play a role Just be yourself

Do whatever you want; no matter what your dream is, go after it. Never do anything you don't enjoy or don't want to do.

Always smile and laugh Always think positively Have confidence in yourself

If you follow these 3 rules

You're living a cosy life.

Congratulations!

What we make

Our art

Main characteristics: three things run through every piece.

Stripes

Stripes are very important to us; we have a very emotional connection to them. Cosy and I only wear stripes. Why? Because to us, stripes are like a path, a well-defined path. A line is a line: it goes straight, never strays from its course and moves forward relentlessly. It has a goal and pursues it no matter what.

Everyone should be like a stripe.

Textiles

We use old clothes and old fabrics, because there's nothing more pleasant than the feel of clothes and fabrics. They keep us warm in winter, like a cosy blanket, or protect us.

A second skin.

Of course: Cosy

Cosy appears in every picture, always wearing stripes or with stripes on the face. Cosy is shown in all kinds of scenarios and situations that explore different themes: sometimes sad and serious ones, but also the very beautiful moments in life, like those of childhood.

“Art is one of the few fields, if not the only one, where you don't have to be perfect. You just have to be yourself.”

Why art

Art is the very embodiment of well-being. The simple fact that anything can be art speaks volumes: there are no rules, you can paint anything. You don't have to be talented, mistakes are allowed, freedom is an absolute priority, and everyone can tell their own story.

That is why we don't really have a defined style. Sometimes we switch things up, sometimes we plan what we do, and sometimes we just let the brush do its thing and see what comes out in the moment.
